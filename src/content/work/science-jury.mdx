In late 2025, I collaborated with HCI researchers at the UCSD Design Lab to create a novel system utilizing multiple AI agents to provide diverse, actionable, and intelligently scaffolded feedback to academic writers drafting up research papers. 

As a UI/UX design intern and the primary designer on this project, I was responsible for designing our experimental and control systems, working with researchers to translate research findings into design goals, and specifying interaction models for our team to build and test against.
